= client

Everything a guest or an operator looks at in a browser lives here.

== The captive portal

This is the page a guest's device is redirected to: the identification questions, the
advertiser video and its survey, and the screen that tells them they are online. It is the only
part of the business a guest ever actually sees, so its wording, its branding and its languages
are what make a location feel like that location rather than a generic form.

== The backoffice's pages

Running the backoffice is mostly a conversation with a model, but a couple of jobs need a page a
person can look at and click through directly: uploading a location's images and videos, and
configuring the venue's WiFi controller. Those pages live here too, embedded inside the
backoffice.

== The maintenance page

While a location is paused — being set up, or taken offline for a reason the venue needs to
explain — this is the page its guests see instead of the portal, so "the WiFi is broken" never
has to be the story.

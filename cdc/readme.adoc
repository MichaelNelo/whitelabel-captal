= cdc

Some things have to happen the moment the database changes, and the change can be made by any
server: a guest who stops playing a replaced video, on any location, frees its file; an error that
shows up on a second guest's phone shortens the wait for everyone who hit it, and tells them so.
This is how the services that react to those changes hear of them, without any of them having to
ask the database over and over, and without any of them depending on the others.

The database reports every change it commits here once; this hands it to every service that
follows along. A service that was away gets what it missed when it comes back, or is told plainly
that it missed too much, so it can look at the database again instead of acting on a partial
picture.

It is internal: nothing outside the deployment reaches it.
